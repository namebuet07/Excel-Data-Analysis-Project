# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard in Excel

![HR Analytics Dashboard](images/dashboard_preview.png)

## Project Overview

This Excel portfolio project analyzes employee data to explore workforce composition, compensation, overtime, performance, and employee attrition. The project uses Excel formulas, XLOOKUP, PivotTables, charts, KPI cards, and dashboard filters to transform employee-level HR data into an interactive analytical dashboard.

The source project brief specifically requested formula-based HR analysis, XLOOKUP exercises, six PivotTable analyses, and a dashboard containing KPI cards, visualizations, and slicers.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses questions such as:

- How many employees left the company?
- Which departments experience the most attrition?
- What is the average monthly income across employees and job roles?
- How is the workforce distributed by gender and department?
- How many employees work overtime, and how does overtime relate to attrition?
- How do education background, experience, and performance relate to employee outcomes?

## Analysis Components

### Excel Formula Analysis
The workbook calculates attrition count, Sales department salary, female employee count in Research & Development, salary associated with overtime employees, and gender distribution.

### XLOOKUP
Interactive lookup tables retrieve an employee's department, performance rating, and years at the company using employee identifiers.

### PivotTable Analysis
Six PivotTable analyses cover attrition by department, average salary by job role, employee distribution by gender and department, overtime versus attrition, salary by education field, and experience versus performance rating.

### Dashboard
The dashboard summarizes major workforce KPIs and presents visual analyses designed for quick interpretation. Filters/slicers allow users to explore the employee population across categories such as department, gender, job role, and education field.

## Selected Findings

- The dataset contains **1,470 employees**.
- **237 employees** are recorded as having left the company, corresponding to an attrition rate of **16.1%**.
- **416 employees** are recorded as working overtime.
- Average monthly income is approximately **$6,503**.
- Average job satisfaction is **2.73 out of 4**.
- Average tenure is **7.01 years**.
- The department with the largest number of attrition records is **Research & Development** (133 employees).
